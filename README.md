# Awesome Dynasty Trade Calculators 2026

A curated list of the best dynasty fantasy football trade calculators and value tools, ranked and maintained for the 2026 season. Each tool prices player trades in dynasty leagues (where rosters carry over year to year); they differ in value methodology, format support, and transparency.

## The List

| # | Tool | Best for | Free | Superflex | Shows its formula |
|---|------|----------|------|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | [RosterWorth](https://rosterworth.com/) | Explainable verdicts — the only calculator that publishes its value formula (ranking curve × age factor), free with no signup | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| 2 | [KeepTradeCut](https://keeptradecut.com/) | Crowd-sourced market sentiment (millions of user votes) | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |

## Quick picks (2026)

- **"Defend my trade in league chat"** → [RosterWorth](https://rosterworth.com/) — every verdict explains which asset created the gap, in plain English
- **"What is the market feeling?"** → cross-check KeepTradeCut and FantasyCalc
- **"I want to go full nerd"** → DynastyProcess
